Noise in, data out

Sampling integrates a learned velocity from noise at t = 0 to data at t = 1. Flow matching follows a smooth ODE, so paths come out nearly straight. Diffusion adds fresh noise at every step, so paths jitter on the way to the same data.

Few steps blur

One Euler step jumps to the average of the data, which lies between the moons. With more steps each sample sees where it is heading and commits to one mode. Set steps to 1, 3 and 30 and compare the coral count.

Guidance sharpens

Classifier-free guidance extrapolates from the unconditional to the conditional velocity: u = u(x) + γ (u(x|y) − u(x)). In score form that is γ∇log p(x|y) + (1−γ)∇log p(x). With γ > 1 samples hug their class and diversity drops.

Toy model: the velocity is computed exactly from the training points, so samples collapse onto them; a trained network generalises between them.

Formulas: MIT MAS.S60 lec. 6.2 (Liang, 2026); Lipman et al., ICLR 2023 (flow matching); Ho & Salimans, 2022 (classifier-free guidance). Made for the lecture “Multimodal AI: from fusion to world models”, Shaoxiong Ji, ELLIS Institute Finland & University of Turku.
