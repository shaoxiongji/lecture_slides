    Every step must land

    One miss ends the episode or sends it down the wrong path. A per-step accuracy of 95% sounds high, yet over 12 steps it leaves about 54% of episodes intact.

    Two kinds of error

    Picking Delete when the task says Submit is a planning error: the agent chose the wrong element. Landing just beside the right button is a grounding error. Dense screens and small targets raise the second kind.

    Why benchmarks disagree

    Single-click grounding benchmarks measure p. Task benchmarks measure roughly pk, plus planning and recovery. A saturated grounding score can still go with low task success.

    Toy model: independent Gaussian click errors on a mock screen. Real agents can sometimes notice a mistake and recover, and errors are often correlated.
